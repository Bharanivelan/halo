1 · Identity & philosophy

Account intelligence for hotel sales, with the evidence attached.

Inhabitr should feel like a well-run sales office: calm, exact and quietly confident. The interface is dense where people work (tables, panels) and generous where they decide (scores, evidence, next actions). One colour means "this is ours"; every other colour means something specific.

Evidence before opinion
Every claim shows how we know it. The Fact / Inference / Unknown marks are the most important visual element in the product.
Calm density
Hairline borders instead of boxes and shadows. Tables at 13px with tabular numerals. Whitespace is used to group things, not to decorate.
Colour carries meaning
Lagoon is the brand and Fit. Marigold is Intent and anything time-sensitive. Iris is reserved for AI Inference. Nothing is coloured just to look nice.
Show the work
Long jobs show their stages, counts, failures and spend while they run. Never a bare spinner.
Warm, not decorative
A serif for page titles and quoted evidence brings a hospitality feel. Everything else is plain and functional.
Mark

A property (centre) inside its target radius. The dashed outer ring is the discovery boundary; the marigold point is a company showing a live signal. This is a working mark until the name is cleared (A-10).

What we avoid
  • gradients
  • glow & sparkles
  • chat-first UI
  • giant KPI cards
  • traffic-light scores
  • decorative illustration
  • shadow on everything
2 · Colour

Linen neutrals, one lagoon accent, and colours that each carry one meaning.

The neutrals lean slightly toward the accent so the product doesn't look like a default grey. Both themes are designed separately: dark mode lightens the accents rather than inverting them.

3 · Typography

A sans for working, a serif for naming and quoting, a mono for machine text.

The serif is used only for page titles, company names in headers and quoted evidence: the places where the product speaks about the world, not about itself. All numbers use tabular figures so columns line up.

Display · NewsreaderAa Rg 173

Page titles, account names, evidence quotes, empty states. Weights 400–600.

Interface · IBM Plex SansAa Rg 173

Everything people read and operate. Engineered, legible at 12–13px, strong tabular numerals. Weights 400, 500, 600.

Data · IBM Plex MonoAa Rg 173

Domains, IDs, logs, costs in run details. Never for body text.

4 · Space, size, radius, elevation, motion

A 4px grid, small radii, and shadows only on things that float.

Surfaces are separated by 1px hairlines. Shadows appear only on layers above the page: menus, popovers, dialogs, drawers and toasts. Radii stay small so dense screens stay crisp.

5 · Navigation & information architecture

Everything happens inside a property.

The property switcher sits at the top of the sidebar because almost every screen is about one hotel. Five working areas sit below it, then system pages. Search and commands live in ⌘K, and a running job is always visible in the top bar.
